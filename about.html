About Me

Youtube

I've been producing, writing, editing and acting for eleven years and have made well over 300 videos. My big break was getting to go do media coverage for DreamHack Denver and Denver Comic-Con. I would go on to get a job at Microsoft for short period of time editing videos for Build and Ignite.

Video Games

I'm a life long video game fan. Been playing as long as I can remember and started getting interested in the games industry by reading magazines like EGM and Game Informer.

Music

Music has always held a special place in my life and I am incredibly knowledgable about all sorts of genres and artists through the years. My taste leans a bit more toward the ecclectic and I'll listen to just about anything once. However, Indie Rock and Hip-Hop are among my favorites.
